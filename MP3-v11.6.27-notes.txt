XSSH v11.6.27 - real-time HDD MP3 streaming

Replaces whole-song preloading with incremental file reads, MP3 decoding, and
48 kHz stereo conversion directly into free nxdk-audio hardware buffers.
There is no 50 MiB PCM allocation and no song rejection based on decoded size.

The two reusable 96 KiB buffers hold up to 1.024 seconds of audio.
The first submission contains 24 KiB (128 ms) to reduce startup delay.
Subsequent submissions use full slots, with short final buffers at EOF.
The current decoder/file remain open during playback and close at track changes.

The MP3 worker requests SDL_THREAD_PRIORITY_TIME_CRITICAL once at startup and
keeps that priority through loading, playback and cleanup. If unsupported,
it requests HIGH. Bounded decoding steps and brief explicit sleeps allow other
threads to run. Disk callbacks check stop/deadline state between operations;
they cannot interrupt a kernel disk read that is already blocked.

Ctrl+Left/Right still select songs. Ctrl+Shift+Left/Right still seek five seconds
from the last completed hardware-buffer position. Forward seeking decodes and
discards incrementally. Backward seeking resets the decoder and advances in
bounded steps; it can take longer late in a long track. Input and cancellation
remain serviced between those steps. No full-file seek-index scan is added.
Volume is applied by the hardware voice.

The loading message clears after the first decoded submission.
A hardware fault keeps the previous driver safeguards and disables audio.
Full app restart may be required after a hardware fault.

Validation includes Xbox-target compilation of the changed audio files, native
buffer ownership/timeouts tests beyond 6 GiB of simulated PCM, and real dr_mp3
and SDL conversion tests for a 330-second stereo MP3, a mono 22.05 kHz track,
rewind, fast-forward and skipping. Native tests use a simulated APU.
Full linked XBE and Xbox hardware playback remain unverified.

XSSH v11.6.8 — rapid skip and backward seek improvements

Based on v11.6.7; no overclocking code.

- Coalesces rapid next/previous presses into one track transition after a
  160 ms quiet interval, reducing SDL audio device churn on the Xbox.
- Adds a sparse dr_mp3 seek table to accelerate backward seeking in VBR MP3s.
- Pauses audio before clearing the queue during seeks and track transitions.
- Preserves 5-second seek increments, MP3 controls and status-bar behavior.

Note: Xbox hardware and full nxdk build have not been verified.

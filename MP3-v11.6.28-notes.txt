XSSH v11.6.28 - fast checkpoint-based rewind

Rewind restores a nearby decoder checkpoint instead of restarting at the beginning
of the MP3. Checkpoints are collected as normal streaming or forward seeking
progresses; no whole-file scan is added to startup or track changes.

Each checkpoint preserves MP3 bit-reservoir/synthesis state and pending decoded
samples. Compressed read-ahead is reread from the saved HDD offset. The existing
incremental seek loop only decodes the remaining distance to the target.

The cache holds at most 128 snapshots (about 4 MiB). It begins with roughly
one-second spacing and thins older checkpoints as long tracks progress, keeping
coverage of the played region. A 5.5-minute test track has roughly four-second
spacing after compaction. Very long tracks have wider checkpoint spacing.
If cache allocation fails, playback continues with the previous slower seek path.
The cache is released on track change or stop. Audio still streams through the
two 96 KiB hardware buffers at the existing maximum worker priority.

Tests: Xbox-target compilation; real CBR, VBR and mono MP3 decoding; exact PCM
comparison against uninterrupted decoding after checkpoint restore and cache
compaction; late-track rewind/forward/skip through the actual worker; double
buffer ownership and timeout checks. No full linked XBE or Xbox hardware test.
The checkpoint implementation depends on the pinned dr_mp3 struct layout;
rerun the PCM comparison test if the decoder dependency changes.
